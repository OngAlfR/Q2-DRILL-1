Beginner's Guide to Badminton

Basic Rules

  • You cannot touch the net
  • 1 Game is equivalent to 21 point
  • Serve underhand or diagonal to opponet
Badminton Equipment

  • Badminton Rackets
  • Shuttlecocks
  • Badminton Nets
Basic Techniques

  • Hold racket like a handshake
  • Stand with feet-shoulder length apart
  • Practice recieve, drop, and smash
